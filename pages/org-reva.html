ReVa Moeders

Neighbourhood mothers in the Regentesse- and Valkenboskwartier, connecting residents through low-threshold activities.

ReVa Moeders started in 2022 as a small group of mothers in the ReVa area (Regentesse- and Valkenboskwartier) of Segbroek and became an official foundation in January 2024. Today the group has about 40 women. It connects neighbourhood residents with low-threshold activities that encourage participation and personal development: weekly language classes, sewing classes and homework support. The group also hands out meals to families with little money. Women who could not attend the language classes for lack of childcare are welcome to bring their children. In 2024 ReVa Moeders received a Gulden Klinker for its social activities in the neighbourhood.
